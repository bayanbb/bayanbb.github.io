How I work

A simple, repeatable process that keeps users at the center and gets ideas into people’s hands fast.

  1. Discover

    Understand the people, their pain points, and how the work really happens today.

  2. Structure

    Map the journeys, roles, and information architecture before drawing a single screen.

  3. Design

    Build the interface in Figma on a consistent system of colors, type, and components.

  4. Build

    Turn the design into a responsive, working web product with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

  5. Refine

    Test with real users, listen closely, and iterate until it feels effortless.
